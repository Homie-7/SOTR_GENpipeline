## What the code does (so you don't have to read it all again)
- `tools/share_previews.py --job whatsapp --only 06_S6` builds the WhatsApp composite from the 1080 show files
  (`3_HD_1080_fallback/1_PLAY_THESE_IN_ORDER/S6_*_wars.mov`). Its layout, captions and encode are `layout`,
  `s6_captions` (it parses `render_s6.py`'s LINES/MOVES with ast), `wa_filter`, `wa_encode` and `wa_scene_cmd`. Sound = map `1:a`
  (the CENTRE file). For the French version: feed it the new 1080 per-wall renders, use Darrin's wav as the audio input,
  the new LINES (French only, from the word table) for the captions, and write a new output name (for example
  `06_S6_wars_FRENCH_review.mp4`) into a new folder. Don't overwrite the existing 06_S6 file.
- In `tools/render_s6.py` every cue is a HARD-CODED old-timeline literal, used across `build()` (washes and words),
  `render_frame`, `draw_m6_v6` and the V7 constants. Recommended retime: a new switch (for example `--retime-fr`) with a
  piecewise-linear map from new time to old time (TMAP, knots taken from the word table), applied in the render loops (`main`,
  `render_final`). The cues follow the warp automatically. To keep physical motion at real speed, the loop textures
  must be fed the REAL time: `GenFlag.frame` (line ~1041, `rd.get((t+offset)*FPS)`), `GenSmoke.draw` (~1183),
  `Smoke.draw` (~1651) and the `sin(t*..)` wobbles. Use phys(t) = REAL_T + (t - T_OLD_NOW), which keeps the +3/+7 offsets.
  Pass dt = real 1/FPS. Put the knots' slope about 1 on burns and motion, and compress the holds (Liberté 79-101 old
  -> about 50.1-57.7 new). Leave DUR old at 165; the new output length is 121.25 s. With the switch OFF, v14 must render bit-identically.
- Fleur-de-lis: the RIGHT flag is `GenFlag(ctx,'R','white',...)` (build, ~line 1856). The colour is `whitec` (`frame()`), and in
  `draw`, `if self.kind=='white': f = whitec`. The cloth coordinates come from `_v6_maps` (uc: the white band is 0..1, the whole
  cloth is about -1..2; rows = v). Lay out a grid of gold fleurs in (uc, v), rasterised from the ⚜ glyph in
  `C:\Windows\Fonts\seguisym.ttf` (U+269C) or drawn as a path. Shade them by `lum/base` like whitec, then run them through
  `_v6_worn` with the cloth. Put this behind the new switch and make the layout an option.
- Ink-in-water ending: replace `MapJourneyV7.morph`'s `alpha = 1 - m` (the V7_ALPHA branch, ~line 1500) under the new switch.
  Proposed: a displacement field = slow curl-noise swirl + a gentle outward push, its magnitude growing with p = ssf(M0,M1,t).
  Remap the colour (mapw) and a density noise field by it, then blur with a sigma that grows with p. Alpha = density:
  ss(clip((1-p)(1+S) - S(1-n_adv))), blurred, which is 1 at p=0 and 0 at p=1. Billowing, thinning, then fully clear.
- Stills: `render_s6.py --stills DIR --at ...` (with V7_ALPHA it writes overSahaj / overGrey PNGs).
